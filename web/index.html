Silent Hill Mobile

Drop your own Silent Hill J2ME JAR here. It's read in this browser and isn't uploaded anywhere.

Click to look around and to continue text, and right-click to go back or open the menu. WASD or the arrow keys take the room's exits. With the gun out, aim at a monster's center and click to shoot. Esc skips the opening, goes back or opens the menu, I opens the inventory and M opens the map.
